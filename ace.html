NSF REU Research · Summer 2025

Cybersecurity training that adapts as fast as the student learns.

ACE is a reinforcement-learning-driven VR system that reads how a learner is performing in real time — mistakes, hesitation, hint use — and reshapes the challenge around them with PPO and AIRL, instead of running everyone through a fixed script.

See how it works Contact me

The Problem

One difficulty curve doesn't fit every learner.

Most VR cybersecurity labs run every student through the same fixed sequence of challenges. Advanced learners get bored and disengage; beginners get stuck without the scaffolding they need — and neither group gets training that's actually calibrated to them.

The Approach

Reading the player, adjusting the lab in real time

Every action in the VR escape room is logged as telemetry. AIRL infers a reward signal from that behavior, and a PPO policy uses it to decide the next difficulty level and hint strategy — closing the loop back into the session live. Hover a stage below for what it does.

Why It Matters

Built for engagement, not just difficulty scaling

Boosts engagement

Personalized pacing keeps advanced learners challenged and beginners supported, instead of losing either group to a fixed curve.

No manual scripting

The lab reshapes itself from live gameplay telemetry — no instructor has to hand-author difficulty tiers per learner.

Explainable by design

Every difficulty and hint decision is logged, so training stays safe, auditable, and easy to reason about after the fact.

Developed as an NSF REU project at the University of Missouri–Columbia; findings presented at the NSF REU showcase.
