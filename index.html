About

I'm a computer science researcher focused on the intersection of artificial intelligence and cybersecurity. My work explores how reinforcement learning (PPO/AIRL), intelligent tutoring systems, and adaptive VR environments can elevate cybersecurity education and defense training. I build systems that analyze player behavior, adapt difficulty dynamically, and provide safe, explainable feedback rooted in RL and neural modeling.

Tools & domains I work with: Python, PyTorch, Unity (VR), SwiftUI, RL pipelines, RAG/guardrails, and secure deployment.
